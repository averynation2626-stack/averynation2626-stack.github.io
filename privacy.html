Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

GTA6 Daily Clips ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What the app stores

When the owner connects their own TikTok or YouTube account, the app receives a login token and stores it only on the owner's own computer. It is used solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect any data from other people.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
  • It does not read other users' content, messages or followers.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in their TikTok or Google account settings and delete the stored token from their computer.
